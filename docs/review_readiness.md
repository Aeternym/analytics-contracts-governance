# Review readiness

A contract can enter review when its business question, counted entity, inclusion and exclusion rules, time boundary, dimensions, owner, approvers, related contracts and acceptance criteria are complete and consistent.

The historical catalog was not ready for approval: metric, dimension and segment records remained `draft`; owner and dashboard catalogs were missing; domain links were empty; and open decisions remained in card text.

The public synthetic examples demonstrate the intended gate. A draft metric may keep open decisions. A review metric must have an owner, valid references and machine-readable acceptance criteria.
